How it works

Three steps, one point of contact.

01 — KICKOFF

We learn the seat

A dedicated hiring manager works out what the role really needs, what your team is like to work in, and which trade-offs you'd actually accept. Written up before anything goes live.

02 — SOURCE & SCREEN

We do the reading

The role goes out to our pipeline and the boards that work for it. We screen every applicant and interview the serious ones, scoring against the brief rather than the résumé.

03 — SHORTLIST

You meet finalists

Three or four candidates with written notes on each — strengths, concerns, compensation, availability. Most searches close inside 21 days, and the hire is guaranteed.

Where founders hire

Hire for the overlap, not the postcode.

The question is never "remote or not" — it's how many hours a day this person needs to be awake at the same time you are. We staff three regions and pick by overlap.

LATIN AMERICA
UTC−3 to UTC−6

Full working-day overlap with US time zones. The default for sales, support and operations seats that need to be live when your customers are.
