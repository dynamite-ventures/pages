How It Works

Built on your infrastructure.
Not ours.

01

Your data stays yours

You connect your own CRM, billing, support, and ops tools. Your data is private — our team never has access to it. The Hive runs inside your company, not ours.

02

Your whole team, inside

Every team member gets access to the shared workspace. Not just the technical ones. Everyone works from the same intelligence layer — with role-based permissions if you need them.

03

Stored on your GitHub

Your company's institutional knowledge — every skill, workflow, and data connection — lives in your own private GitHub repository. You own it. You control it. It goes with you.

04

AI provider of your choice

Your Hive works with Claude, GPT, Gemini — whichever provider offers the best rates or capabilities at any given time. When providers change their pricing or deprecate models, your Hive is unaffected.

Hive Minded is a layer on top of Claude Code — the same infrastructure we built for Dynamite Ventures, installed inside your company. The AI changes. Your Hive doesn't.
