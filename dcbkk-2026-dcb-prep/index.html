Saturday Masterminds

How to Prepare

Saturday's masterminds are the heart of DC BLACK Day. This year you'll have two mastermind sessions, each with a different focus. Come prepared for both. The more thought you put in beforehand, the more you'll take away — and the more valuable you'll be to your group.

01

Your Biggest Challenge or Opportunity

Use this session to get real, focused feedback on something that matters. What's the one problem, decision, or opportunity you most want to leave Bangkok having worked through?

This could be

  • · A strategic challenge you've been sitting on
  • · A decision you can't seem to make
  • · An opportunity you're not sure how to capture
  • · Anything personal or professional where a sharp group of entrepreneurs would help you think more clearly

To Prepare

Spend time before Saturday thinking this through. Use the Hot Seat Template below to clarify your thinking. Walk into the room knowing exactly what you want to say — and what you want to walk away with.

02

How Could You Double the Business in 12 Months?

The second session is designed to be a little looser and more generative. The question on the table: what would it take to double your business in the next 12 months?

This isn't about a safe, incremental plan. Come ready to explore a bold strategy, a new concept, or a radical change you've been thinking about but haven't fully stress-tested. The group's job is to push you, poke holes in it, and help you see angles you're missing.

To Prepare

Think about what "doubling" actually means for your business right now — revenue, team, impact, or something else. Come with at least one real idea or direction you'd be willing to put on the table. It doesn't have to be polished. That's what the group is for.

Mastermind Principles

Every session opens with these

01

What's said here, stays here.

Trust is our foundation. Real conversations are fueled by being open and vulnerable. Do not record any sessions — this includes wearable AI recorders and any other devices.

02

Challenge with questions, not suggestions.

Great insights come from great questions. Help each other think and be curious — don't just give answers.

03

Show up on time, every time.

You've made a commitment to each other. Be present and ready to contribute.

04

We're here for the same reason.

To learn and grow together as entrepreneurs.
