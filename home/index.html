FAQ

How can I join?
Check that you meet the membership requirements and agree to the Code of Conduct. Submit your application to join here.

You'll answer a few questions about yourself and your business so we can ensure you're the right culture fit. If you know a current DC member, make sure to include their name in your application. A member of our team will personally review your application and notify you of your acceptance within 48 business hours.

You'll receive login access to the Dynamite Circle forum and an invitation to a group Welcome Call so you can start making connections right away.

What's the cost of the membership?
A one-time payment of $697 gives you yearlong, unlimited access to connections with over 1,400 global founders running remote businesses.

You'll participate in vetted members-only events around the world, the private forum, facilitated calls with other members, peer masterminds, topical WhatsApp groups, and local meet-ups.

What do you get with your membership?
Your Dynamite Circle membership gives you access to a global network of location-independent entrepreneurs, and opportunities to collaborate and create relationships with each other in-person and online, including:
  • A private platform with discussion forums and AI-powered member matching
  • Virtual masterminds on business topics like SaaS growth, e-commerce, mindset, and productized services
  • Locator tool to find other DCers in your area and share your travel plans
  • Access to Juntos (in-person local meetups) in multiple cities worldwide
  • Access to members-only DC events, including DCBKK, DC Mexico, and regional DCX events
  • Monthly Connect Calls — quick, one-on-one chats with other members
  • Daily active WhatsApp groups for each chapter city, plus business and lifestyle themes

What are Dynamite Circle events like?
Members say DC events are their favourite part of the membership. These gatherings are cheat codes, helping you level up in your business, connections, mindset, and leadership. All-DC conferences happen twice a year in Mexico (summer) and Bangkok (winter), plus member-led DC Adventures, DCX events, and local meet-ups.

Not only do we discuss business through targeted workshops, keynote speakers, and masterminds, we also enjoy local cuisine and fun activities. Tickets are available for purchase for members only. To accommodate nomad lifestyles, many DC events allow you to bring your spouse and kids.

See the event schedule here.

What is DC BLACK?
DC BLACK is an extension of the DC membership, exclusively for 7- and 8-figure founders. You still have access to the wider community and all its features, along with a backstage pass to high-level conversations in a more intimate small group setting.
  • Private WhatsApp group with daily conversations, questions, and business shortcuts
  • Facilitated masterminds led by a team of vetted experts
  • Virtual learning sessions on business topics like hiring and operations
  • Exclusive DC BLACK-only events in Phuket, Barcelona, and NYC each year
  • VIP access to our all-DC events in Mexico and Bangkok
Learn more here.

What makes Dynamite Circle different from other business communities?
Many current DCers left other business memberships to join the DC. What sets us apart is our culture of generosity and helpfulness. We welcome business owners from all corners of the globe, with all sorts of unique hobbies, quirks, and lifestyles. The DC is a place where you can nerd out over your niches and find other people who are just as interested.

Members have shown up for each other in so many ways, not just in business. We've had DCers receive tangible and emotional support through loss of a home, divorce, and other personal struggles. It's rare to find a community that actually cares for each other, and that's what we've cultivated inside the DC.

How soon can I start meeting entrepreneurs?
Right away! As soon as you join, you'll gain access to our member platform, where you can connect with other remote founders, join a mastermind, or hop on a Connect Call. You'll also be invited to a group Welcome Call, where you'll meet other new members and get a tour of your DC experience.

Offline, you can meet DC members in person at Juntos (member-organized local meetups), chapter events like DCX London, and at our major annual conferences DCBKK and DC Mexico.

What types of businesses are in the community?
Dynamite Circle is home to self-made entrepreneurs running 6, 7, and 8-figure businesses in industries like SaaS & Tech, e-commerce (DTC brands, Amazon FBA, Shopify), productized services, courses & content, and more.

Members are serious about business but love adventure — whether it's deep diving into a new market, expanding to new regions, or simply finding a great co-working spot in a new city.

Who does not get accepted to the community?
Dynamite Circle is an application-based community for location-independent entrepreneurs running established businesses. While we welcome ambitious founders at different stages, we don't accept:
  • Business coaches, lifestyle coaches, brokers, recruiters, consultants, or freelancers
  • Founders with businesses under $100k in annual revenue or notable exits
  • People who want to use the community to pitch or sell their business
  • People who don't vibe with our culture of helpfulness and generosity

What's a mastermind?
Masterminds are virtual, member-led sessions where DCers deep-dive into specific business topics. Whether it's TikTok Shop growth, hiring international talent, or email marketing, you can join or start a Mastermind to exchange strategies and solutions with fellow founders.

What's a Junto?
Juntos are in-person meetups hosted by DC members in cities around the world. These are casual gatherings — grabbing drinks, dinner, or doing a fun cultural activity together.

When do Connect Calls happen?
Connect Calls take place according to a regular schedule, available in North America and Europe time zones. These are quick, one-on-one chats designed to introduce you to someone new in the community.
