Dynamite Circle is for you if…

You run a location-independent business generating at least $100,000 in annual revenue.

You are eager to meet other founders who you can share your lifestyle and activities with.

You’re a self-proclaimed business or internet nerd, with unique interests outside of work.

You agree to uphold our Code of Conduct and Community Guidelines.

You’re excited about growing and evolving as a leader and human being, while helping and supporting other business owners on the same path.

You share our enthusiasm for traveling and meeting up with other founders around the world.

Dynamite Circle is not for you if…

You’re a business coach, lifestyle coach, broker, recruiter, consultant, or freelancer.

You intend to pitch or sell your business to the community, or discuss topics of contention.

You’re not fully committed to your business growth, and the growth of your fellow DC members.

You’re not interested in leaving your home office and are comfortable staying stuck exactly where you are.

Connecting global-minded entrepreneurs for over 15 years

We know how isolating and discouraging it can be to face these challenges on your own. We’ve been doing it since 2009, starting with an ecommerce company that had outsourced talent and no physical office — way before remote work and location independence became mainstream.

We wanted to meet other entrepreneurs who were doing cool, innovative things like us, so we started the Tropical MBA podcast to connect with other founders in the internet space. In 2011, we got together with 20 listeners of the pod on a remote, tropical island — and Dynamite Circle was born. We still drop new episodes of the pod every week to give away free game and share what we’ve learned over the last decade and change.
