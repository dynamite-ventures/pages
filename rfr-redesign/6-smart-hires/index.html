6 Smart Hires You Need on Your Team

To optimize your time and scale your company.

01

Operations / Project Manager

5+ years remote / SaaS / Ops experience

Responsibilities

  • —Streamline processes and create SOPs
  • —Hire, onboard, and train staff
  • —Establish KPIs and measure performance
  • —Manage day-to-day operations
  • —Resolve issues proactively
  • —Serve as strategic business partner

Starting Salaries

Region Salary
Asia $24k+
Latin America $40k+
Europe $42k+
United States $70k+

Customer Success Manager

3+ years remote / SaaS / CSM experience

Responsibilities

  • —Create processes and CSM playbook
  • —Manage team and KPIs
  • —Handle customer support across email, chat, and Zoom
  • —Lead product demos
  • —Provide proactive troubleshooting

Starting Salaries

Region Salary

Digital Marketing Manager

  • —Manage SEO, email, social, and paid ads
  • —Build and grow the marketing team
  • —Handle automation and CRM
