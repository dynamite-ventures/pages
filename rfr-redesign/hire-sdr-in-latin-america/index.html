What's an SDR?

Sales Development Representatives are the engine of your outbound sales pipeline — keeping your calendar full of qualified conversations.

  • Qualified appointment setting and creating new sales opportunities
  • Finding and qualifying your ideal customer
  • Setting meetings with founders or sales professionals
  • Also referred to as BDRs or appointment setters
