How it works

Four steps, one point of contact.

01 — KICKOFF

We learn the seat

We work closely with you to understand the role, your company culture, and what makes someone a great fit for your team. We take the time to learn your priorities, identify the key skills and experience needed, and build a clear picture of your ideal candidate before the search begins. We approach every search with the same mindset we use when hiring for our own team: finding talented, reliable people who have the skills, attitude, and drive to make a real impact.

02 — SOURCE & SCREEN

We do the reading

We get the word out, tap into our network, and reach out to people who could be a great fit. We review applications, interview candidates, and take the time to really get to know them beyond their resumes. We look at their experience, communication, personality, and what they're looking for in their next role. Our goal is to find people who not only have the right skills but are the kind of people we'd be excited to hire ourselves.

03 — SHORTLIST

You meet finalists

We introduce you to candidates we believe would be a great fit for your team. You'll get a helpful summary of each person, giving you a better understanding of their background, experience, and why we think they're worth meeting. You decide who you'd like to interview, and we'll help coordinate everything along the way. We'll also stay involved, gather feedback, and keep things moving until you find someone you're excited to bring on board.

04 — OUR GUARANTEE

We stand behind every hire

It's rare that a hire doesn't work out, but we know it can happen. That's why every placement comes with a 90-day replacement guarantee. If things don't work out, we'll find you a replacement at no additional recruiting fee. We want you to feel confident in every person you bring onto your team.

Where founders hire

Global talent. Built around your team.

Every region has its strengths, and knowing where to look for specific roles can make all the difference in hiring the right team.

LATIN AMERICA

UTC−3 to UTC−6

Full working-day overlap with US time zones. The default for strong engineering, customer/ sales support, and operations seats that need to be live when your customers are.

Salaries from $2,000/month

EUROPE

UTC+0 to UTC+3

Morning overlap with the US East Coast, full overlap with UK and EU customers. Strong for account management, marketing and engineering.

Salaries from $2,500/month

NORTH AMERICA

UTC−4 to UTC−8

Same-day, same-hours. Where the seat needs deep domain knowledge of the US market or customer-facing seniority.

Salaries from $3,500/month
