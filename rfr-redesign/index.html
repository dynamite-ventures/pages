The problem

Most recruiters don't understand what remote-ready actually means.

They send resumes from candidates who have never worked async, can't manage their own schedule, or fold under the autonomy of distributed teams. The result is a hire that looks great on paper and struggles in practice.

Remote First Recruiting has operated 100% remotely since 2012. We know what remote-ready looks like — because we live it. We test for the things traditional recruiters ignore.

The process

Three steps to your next hire

01

Discovery call

We learn your team culture, timezone preferences, tech stack, and what remote-ready means for your company. A real conversation, not a generic intake form.

02

Sourcing & vetting

We tap our global network and run every candidate through our remote-readiness framework — testing for async communication, self-management, and autonomy, not just technical skills.

03

Shortlist & hire

You receive 3–5 pre-vetted, pre-interviewed candidates. No volume dumps. We stay involved through offer and onboarding so nothing slips.
